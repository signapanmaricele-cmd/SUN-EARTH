☀️ The Sun’s Influence on Earth

Understanding how the Sun shapes life, weather, and energy on our planet

Life Weather Seasons Energy

🌱 The Sun and Life on Earth

The Sun is the main source of energy for all living things. Plants use sunlight to produce food through photosynthesis, which also provides oxygen for humans and animals.

🌦️ The Sun and Weather

The Sun heats the Earth's surface unevenly, causing wind, clouds, and rainfall. It drives weather patterns and helps form oceans currents and storms.

🍂 The Sun and Seasons

Earth's tilt and its orbit around the Sun cause the seasons. When sunlight is more direct, it is summer. When it is less direct, it becomes winter.

⚡ Solar Energy

The Sun provides renewable energy that humans can use through solar panels. It is clean, unlimited, and helps reduce pollution.
